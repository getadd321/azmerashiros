Our Story

A table set with tradition

Azmera Shiro is a traditional Ethiopian restaurant specializing in delicious shiro, fasting foods, traditional dishes, seed juices, and catering services for weddings, meetings, birthdays, and special events.

  • Homemade shiroSlow-ground berbere and chickpea flour, simmered daily
  • Vegan-friendly menuFull vegan & vegeterian dishes, always available
  • Full-service cateringWeddings, meetings, birthdays & special events
